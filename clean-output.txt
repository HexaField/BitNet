Question: What is quantum computing?
Answer: Quantum computing is a new type of computer that uses quantum mechanics to perform calculations. Unlike classical computers that use bits to store information as either 0 or 1, quantum computers use quantum bits, or qubits, which can exist in multiple states simultaneously. This allows quantum computers to process a vast amount of information and perform complex calculations much faster than classical computers.  The principle behind quantum computing is based on the laws of quantum mechanics, which govern the behavior of particles at the atomic and subatomic levels.
